## FAQ (The Real Questions)

**1. Is this actually free or is there a catch?**
It's 100% free for the self-service audit. We build this because it's the best way to show you that Credex knows our stuff. If you have a huge team ($5k+/mo spend) and want us to handle the actual negotiations and credit sourcing for you, we have a paid service for that. But the tool itself is free forever.

**2. Do I have to connect my bank account or AWS?**
Nope. I hate tools that ask for my Plaid login. You just tell us how many seats you have on each plan. It takes 2 minutes and nothing ever touches your sensitive financial data.

**3. How do I know your pricing data is actually right?**
I manually verify every price in our database against the official vendor pages (Cursor, Anthropic, OpenAI, etc.) every single week. If a price changes on a Tuesday, it’s usually in our engine by Wednesday morning.

**4. What if we’re on a custom "Enterprise" plan?**
The engine will flag it as a "Negotiated" tier. It won’t know your exact secret price, but it will compare your spend against the industry benchmarks we’ve collected to tell you if you’re getting a good deal or if you're being overcharged.

**5. Can I export the results to show my boss?**
Yep. Once you finish the audit, you get a unique URL you can share, and if you drop your email, we'll send you a clean summary you can forward to your CFO so you look like a hero for saving the company money.
