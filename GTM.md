# Go-To-Market Strategy (GTM)

## Target User
Engineering Managers, CTOs, and Founders at seed-to-series-B startups (5-50 person teams). Specifically those without a dedicated procurement department who are likely overpaying for multiple AI seats.

## Channels
- **Product Hunt:** Launching as a free "utility tool" for the AI community.
- **LinkedIn:** Sharing "Savings Benchmarks" for different startup stages.
- **Twitter/X:** Building in public and sharing aggregate spend insights.
- **Cold Outreach:** Using the audit results as a hook for Credex procurement services.
