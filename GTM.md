# Go-To-Market Strategy (GTM)

### The Target User (The "Stuck" Engineering Manager)
Our primary persona is the **Engineering Manager or CTO at a seed-to-series-B SaaS startup (10–40 person team)**.
- **The Pain Point:** They are spending $1,500–$4,000/month on AI tools. They know they have duplicate seats (some devs on Cursor, some on Copilot), but they don't have the "finance bandwidth" to spend 3 hours auditing it.
- **The Trigger:** A monthly credit card bill that just jumped 20% due to an "Enterprise" upsell or a "Team" seat minimum they didn't realize they triggered.

### Distribution Channels (Non-Trivial)

#### 1. The "Benchmark Leak" (Cold Outreach Hook)
Instead of a generic "Save money on SaaS" email, Credex sends a highly specific one-liner: *"We benchmarked 50 teams in the [Industry] space, and the average AI spend per dev is $142. Based on your team size, you might be overpaying by ~$500/mo. Run the 2-minute audit here."*

#### 2. Niche Slack/Discord Communities
Targeting specific "Power User" groups:
- **Rands Leadership Slack:** (EMs/CTOs). Posting as a "Utility Tool for the community."
- **YC Bookface:** Specifically targeting the current batch of seed founders who are scaling their AI stack for the first time.
- **Elixir/Rust/Python Discords:** Posting in the #tooling channels where devs are debating Cursor vs Copilot.

#### 3. High-Value "Social Proof" Partnerships
Partnering with fractional CFOs who manage 5–10 startups each. We give them a "Partner Dashboard" (referral_code tracking) where they can run audits for all their clients in one go, positioning them as "heroes" for finding instant savings.

### The First 100 Users ($0 Budget Plan)
**Days 1-10: The Direct 50**
- Identify 50 seed-stage CTOs on LinkedIn.
- Send a personalized loom showing the "Benchmark Card" and how it works.
- Goal: 15 audits completed.

**Days 11-20: The Community Wedge**
- Post a "State of AI Spend 2026" summary on Hacker News and Reddit (/r/startups).
- Use aggregate (mocked) data to show common waste patterns (e.g., "Why 40% of teams overpay for Claude API").
- Goal: 50 audits completed.

**Days 21-30: The Referral Loop**
- Use the "Share your audit results" feature.
- Incentivize the first 50 users to share their results (privately) with one other founder to compare benchmarks.
- Goal: 35 audits completed.

### Week-1 Traction Metrics
- **Audits Completed:** 25
- **Lead Capture Rate:** >15% (Targeting high-intent users).
- **Viral Coefficient:** 0.2 (1 in 5 users shares the audit with a colleague).
- **North Star:** "Identified Savings" ($ found / month). This metric directly proves the tool's value to both the user and Credex.
